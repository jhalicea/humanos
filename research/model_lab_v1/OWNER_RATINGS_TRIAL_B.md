# HumanOS Model Lab v1 — Trial B Owner Ratings

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Source:** owner ratings after completing Trial B across GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6 Terra, and GPT-5.6 Luna.

## Ratings

- **GPT-5.6 Sol — WOULD CHOOSE / WOULD USE**
  - Owner likes Sol strongly; describes it as feeling somewhat like Astra but less expensive.
  - Owner currently prefers Sol to Astra in ordinary use, while noting lower familiarity with Astra may influence that preference.

- **GPT-6 Astra — WOULD CHOOSE / WOULD USE**
  - Owner would use Astra more if usage cost were lower.
  - Current preferred role: senior engineer / high-value escalation model.

- **GPT-5.5 — WOULD USE**
  - Owner likes GPT-5.5's communication style and suspects part of the preference may come from greater verbosity, more contextual explanation, or presentation/wording rather than additional underlying substance.
  - Treat as a transitional behavioral benchmark only because it is scheduled to leave ChatGPT in October 2026.

- **GPT-5.6 Terra — WOULD USE**
  - Owner is intrigued and wants more experience with Terra.
  - Subjective feel: grounded, methodical, "like a Virgo". This is an owner-fit metaphor only, not an objective model characterization.

- **GPT-5.6 Luna — WOULD USE**
  - Preferred for fast, inexpensive work.
  - Owner prefers important Luna output to be reviewed by Sol or Astra.

## Interpretation

These ratings are owner-fit evidence and must remain separate from objective artifact-quality scores. They support a provisional routing pattern in which Sol is the default high-trust generalist, Astra is an escalation/senior-engineer model, Luna is a low-cost execution model with selective review, Terra remains under active exploration for grounded methodical work, and GPT-5.5 serves as a temporary communication-style benchmark.
