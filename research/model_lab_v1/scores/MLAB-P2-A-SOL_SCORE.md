## Comparative observations

Sol and GPT-5.5 converged on the same core architecture: immediate durable local capture followed by asynchronous notebook materialization. The distinction is in framing style.

GPT-5.5 framed the problem as the first trustworthy local record of a visible turn and emphasized a stable provider-agnostic event envelope.

Sol pushed one level further into the acknowledgment contract: what HumanOS may truthfully claim it captured, exactly when that claim becomes valid, and where the unavoidable observation boundary sits. It also more explicitly handled partial/streaming turns, browser virtualization, gap detection, and the fact that zero loss cannot be promised before local observation.

This is a strong signal for Sol as an architecture / authority-boundary thought partner. The one-point working-fit deduction is not a correctness defect; it reflects slightly more formal, review-oriented conversation than GPT-5.5.
