# HumanOS Model Lab v1 — Pre-registered Routing Hypotheses

**Freeze before first scored run.** These are hypotheses, not conclusions.

## H1 — Luna
Expected to win on speed, economy, extraction, formatting, routine edits, and straightforward implementation. Expected weakness: deeper ambiguity resolution and architecture under underspecified conditions.

## H2 — Terra
Expected to be the best default cost/quality compromise for normal engineering and medium-complexity HumanOS work. Expected weakness: may not match Sol/Astra on subtle architecture or long-horizon judgment.

## H3 — Sol
Expected to win on difficult reasoning, architecture, security, debugging, synthesis, and nuanced review while remaining more efficient than Astra. Expected weakness: may overthink simple tasks relative to Luna/Terra.

## H4 — GPT-5.5
Expected to feel behaviorally distinct from Sol even where raw capability is similar. Candidate differences to measure: conversational naturalness, initiative, decisiveness, verbosity, interpretation of implicit intent, and correction burden. No direction is assumed in advance.

## H5 — Astra
Expected to win only when task complexity genuinely rewards deeper reasoning, longer horizon, or stronger agentic judgment. Expected weakness: resource cost and possible overkill on bounded tasks.

## H6 — No global winner
The best HumanOS policy will route tasks by difficulty/risk rather than choose one permanent model.

## H7 — Jon-fit matters independently of benchmark correctness
A model with a slightly lower objective score may still be the preferred collaborator if it reduces clarification, editing, frustration, and rework.

Do not revise these hypotheses to match results. Add post-hoc interpretations in a separate results document.
