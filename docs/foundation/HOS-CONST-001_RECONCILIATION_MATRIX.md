## Disposition vocabulary

- **PRESERVE** — carry forward with no material change other than status/context wording.
- **CLARIFY** — preserve principle while narrowing ambiguity or making a current boundary explicit.
- **ADD** — candidate constitutional protection not explicit enough in the historical text.
- **CONSOLIDATE** — later source is substantially already covered; retain concise constitutional form and leave details subordinate.
- **DEFER** — keep as subordinate governance/implementation issue rather than constitutionalize now.
- **HISTORICAL ONLY** — preserve as history, not current local status.

## Net candidate changes

Compared with the historically ratified August Constitution, v0.2-rc1 should remain intentionally concise. The reconciliation proposes only a small set of constitutional-level changes:

1. explicit local-first/current-local-authority boundary while preserving historical ratification as provenance;
2. explicit third-party dignity / shared-data protection;
3. stronger truthful-status and false-memory language;
4. Mirror changed from **sole** human-facing interface to **primary continuity/executive identity**, preserving governed recovery/admin paths;
5. bounded emergency-stay requirements;
6. explicit separation of governing Instruments, source evidence, representations, and derivatives at principle level;
7. explicit reviewer/FRIENDS non-authority;
8. explicit model self-certification prohibition;
9. explicit scarcity rule: routing may degrade, rights may not;
10. historical readiness statuses not inherited by the local re-bootstrap.
