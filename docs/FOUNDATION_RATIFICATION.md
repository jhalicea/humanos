# HumanOS Foundation Contract v0.1 — Ratification Record

Status: RATIFIED BY JON
Date: 2026-09-11
Implementation status: Incremental implementation; not all contracts are implemented.

This record ratifies the Round 5 amendments while preserving the original foundation candidate.

Core commitments:

- Human ownership and explicit approval for consequential actions.
- Life Notebook as chronological evidence, separate from canonical state and derived views.
- Models and FRIENDS may propose but cannot authorize or commit state.
- Privacy operations must be owner-authorized, receipted, and truthful about external copies.
- Erased or hidden content must not re-enter indexes, caches, adapters, or model-training corpora.
- Self-editing is permitted only through isolated branches, deterministic policy checks, tests, review, and human approval.
- Provider neutrality, local-first operation, recovery, provenance, and rollback remain mandatory.

Ratification authorizes implementation planning. It does not claim that every contract or capability is currently implemented.
